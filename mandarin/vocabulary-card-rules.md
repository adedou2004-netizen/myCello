# Vocabulary card rules

## Appearance

- Make a portrait PNG with a warm cream background and rounded, softly colored sections. Use the vocabulary-card examples, such as `apple_ping2-guo3.png`, as a visual reference.
- Put the simplified Chinese headword in very large type next to one focused illustration. Below the headword, show the pinyin with tone marks, one short English meaning, and a brief French equivalent when available.
- Keep Characters and Mnemonic easy to scan. Include History when a brief, sourced account adds something. Add an Example sentence when one is available, with Chinese, pinyin, and an English translation.
- Render all lettering with fonts. Ask the local image model for a warm storybook scene with a clear subject and a simple background. Check the finished preview for glyphs, pinyin, legibility, and stray lettering in the illustration.

## Character analysis and mnemonics

- Explain each character in order. Give its reading and its role in the word. For a compound, distinguish what a character means on its own from what it contributes to the word.
- Describe actual written components and where they sit. Identify semantic and phonetic roles when supported. A phonetic component can give a historical sound clue even when the modern readings differ.
- Verify historical claims and record a source when making one. A modern visual breakdown can stand on its own. For a simple form like 一, describe the stroke directly.
- Use one concrete, memorable scene for the word. Connect the scene to the meaning or to a recognizable component in a short sentence. Present invented associations under **Mnemonic**.

## Filename

- Use `english-meaning_numbered-pinyin.png`, for example `strawberry_cao3-mei2.png`.
- Use lowercase ASCII, one underscore between the meaning and pinyin, and hyphens between English words and between pinyin syllables. Use `v` for ü and `5` for the neutral tone. Keep tone numbers in the filename and tone marks on the card.
- Use a single English meaning. If the filename already exists, save the new card with `-v2`, `-v3`, and so on before `.png`.
- Save approved PNGs in the Windows Downloads folder under `Mandarin-Cards`.
